# StoneAge Online v0.1-11

## Pet management

- `0x0901` PetListRequest
- `0x0902` PetListResponse
- `0x0903` PetActivateRequest
- `0x0904` PetActivateResponse
- `0x0905` PetRenameRequest
- `0x0906` PetRenameResponse
- `0x0907` PetReleaseRequest
- `0x0908` PetReleaseResponse

Rules:
- Pet management requires `SessionState.InWorld`.
- A character can have only one active battle pet.
- Renamed pet names are trimmed, max 24 characters, and reject control characters.
- Active pet selection is transactional and automatically deactivates the previous pet.
- Captured pet limit remains 5 per character.

## Battle pet

The active pet is snapshotted when a random encounter begins.

Turn order for the current foundation:
1. Player action.
2. Active pet attacks if alive and loyalty check succeeds.
3. Monster attacks player if still alive.

Loyalty obedience chance:

`clamp(50 + loyalty / 2, 50, 100)%`

Pet victory EXP equals the monster EXP reward.

Pet EXP required for next level:

`level * 80`

Pet level-up growth:
- MaxHP +6 and fully heal
- Attack +2
- Defense +1
- Agility +1
- Loyalty +1 after a victory, capped at 100

## TestClient

Commands:
- `recv`
- `attack`
- `defend`
- `escape`
- `capture`
- `pets`
- `petactive <petId>`
- `petname <petId> <name>`
- `petrelease <petId>`

`recv` is intentionally available because movement can produce both `MoveBroadcast` and a subsequent asynchronous `BattleStart` packet.

## Acceptance

1. Capture a pet and verify it persists in `character_pets`.
2. `pets` lists the captured pet and its stats.
3. `petactive <id>` makes exactly one pet active.
4. Rename persists across reconnect.
5. Released pet is deleted and cannot be selected again.
6. Start an encounter with an active pet and verify `BattleStart` includes pet data.
7. Pet can add damage after the player action.
8. Low loyalty can cause a pet to skip its attack.
9. Victory grants pet EXP and can level the pet.
10. Pet management packets are ignored while the session is `InBattle`.
